New-Parent Welcome Pack

Just admitted? Follow these 6 steps and your child is fully settled.

1

Visit the Office

Come within 2 weeks with your admission slip to collect your file and class allocation.

2

Pay School Fees

Pay by bank transfer to the school account and collect your official receipt number.

3

Get Uniform

Buy the full uniform set at the school shop. See the Uniform List.

4

Portal Login

Create your password on the Portal with your child's registration number.

5

Meet the Teacher

Introduce yourself to the class teacher and share any health or feeding notes.

6

First Day

Arrive before 7:45am with school bag, lunch, and a big smile!
