Staff Code of Conduct

TREASURE KIDDIES CENTRE, AGEVA
opposite Morak Pure Water Factory

Staff Code of Conduct

  1. The school resumes 7:30am and closes 3:00pm. All staff are expected to be in school before 7:30am and see to the exit of their pupils before the close of the day.
  2. All movement of staff — resumption and closing — with reason(s) and time must be documented in the movement book, for the acknowledgment of the Headmistress.
  3. All teachers are expected to have their timetable and adhere to it strictly.
  4. Movement from one’s class, and the use of handsets for selfish purposes rather than for the school’s work, is highly prohibited.
  5. Extorting money from parents through the pupils shall attract instant dismissal.
  6. All conducts, direct or indirect, that aims to dent the image and credibility of the school shall attract instant dismissal without notice.
  7. All financial misconduct by any staff is highly prohibited.
  8. Lateness to school after the stipulated time shall attract financial penalties, at the management’s prerogative.
  9. Absence from school without cogent reason(s) made known to the management shall attract dismissal without payment.
  10. No staff is expected to engage in any other trade or business within school hours, except as management views it, and it must be to the benefit of the school.
  11. Fighting, quarrelling, insubordinate acts against your superior, and gossiping shall attract a stiff penalty as may be viewed by management.
  12. No staff is allowed to leave the school premises for the purpose of break-fast.
  13. One month’s notice of any staff exit shall be conveyed to the proprietor and through the Headmistress; and all documents, properties and financial indebtedness shall be redeemed before exit.
  14. The school maintains a code of dressing — no religious or tribal dressing is allowed, and the wearing of slippers is highly prohibited unless management allows otherwise.
  15. All tender/suckling babies are expected to be dropped at the day-care class.
  16. Payment of salaries commences from the first day of entry through the days that make a month; we are not liable to pay Saturdays and Sundays, but public holidays during the school session shall be paid.
  17. Holding lessons is compulsory, especially during long vacations; the benefit of and payment for such duties shall be communicated to you through management.
  18. No staff is expected to engage in any business with parents; if found wanting, management will view it as gross misconduct and treat it accordingly.
  19. Lesson plans must be prepared ahead of every day’s teaching and not during the teaching period; the Headmistress shall cross-check them for marking. Failure to prepare the said lesson plan shall attract financial punishment.
  20. Salary increase shall be the sole prerogative of management, through hard work and dedication to duty. Management will not entertain any form of staff association/union; participation in such shall be viewed as conspiracy and shall be punished accordingly.

Sign — Management  ·
