Staff Code of Conduct

Treasure Academy · opposite Morak Pure Water Factory, Ageva. These are the working rules of the school. Every staff member reads and accepts them.

1. School hours

School opens 7:30am and closes 3:00pm. Every staff must be in school before 7:30am and see to the exit of their pupils before the day ends.

2. Movement book

Resumption, closing and every movement of staff must be documented, with reasons given to the Headmistress.

3. Stay with your class

No teacher leaves their class for personal errands or handset use during lessons. A class must never be without its teacher.

4. No money from pupils

Collecting money from pupils or parents, for any reason, is strictly prohibited.

5. Protect the school name

All conduct, direct or indirect, that dents the image and credibility of the school is punishable.

6. Money matters

All financial decisions belong to the Headmistress and management alone. Lateness to duty carries a penalty.

7. Absence

Any absence must come with serious, provable reasons, communicated to management before or immediately after.

8. No side business in school hours

Engaging in any trade or business within school hours, other than your teaching duties, is prohibited.

9. No rough treatment

No insulting, shouting at or physically correcting any pupil, parent or fellow staff, anywhere on school premises.

10. Keep school records inside

Pupils records, reports and school documents must not be given out or photographed for outsiders.

11. Dressing

Dress smartly and modestly every day. Slippers are not allowed in class. Traditional dress is welcome on Fridays.

12. Health & leave

Sick or emergency days must be reported at once. Leave does not attract pay; duty on Saturdays and planned days attracts normal pay.

13. No secret meetings

No staff gathering, class-to-class campaign or teachers meeting without the Headmistress or management present.

14. Extra duties

Lesson notes must be ready before classes and checked by the Headmistress daily. Duties given outside normal teaching - events, trips, checking books - are part of the work.

15. Salary

Salary is considered by management alone, on the last working day of the month, with good conduct and hard work in view.

16. Associations

Any association, union or group formed within the school against this order is treated as conspiracy and punished.

17. Employment

Employment may be ended by management where conduct or duty is found wanting. Service is a privilege, kept by character and hard work.

— Management ·
