Fatimah the Brilliant Girl

Once upon a time, there lived a girl called Fatimah Abubakar Zanna.

Fatimah was a brilliant girl who helped to teach her friends at school.

One day, she told her mother, "Mummy, someday I will be a doctor, in shaa Allah."

Fatimah taught her friends many things.

One day, she came back from school and said, "Mummy, I think I want to become a pilot."

Her mother said, "Fatimah, do as you wish. I am proud of you."

Fatimah was very happy and danced all day.

The end.
